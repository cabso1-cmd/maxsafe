Documentation & Trade Compliance

Accurate trade documentation is as important as the physical survey. MaxSafe helps verify the paperwork behind your cargo movements.

Discrepancies in shipping documentation can be as costly as physical cargo loss. MaxSafe supports cargo owners and freight forwarders in verifying and coordinating the documentation that underpins a shipment.

What this service covers

  • Bill of lading verification — confirming bill of lading details align with cargo actually shipped.
  • Letter of credit verification — reviewing shipping documentation against letter of credit requirements.
  • Freight forwarding coordination — supporting the logistics and documentation side of a shipment.
  • Regulatory & risk compliance audits — reviewing shipping operations against applicable regulatory requirements.

Who this is for

Cargo owners, freight forwarders, and importers/exporters who need independent verification of trade documentation alongside — or separate from — a physical cargo survey.
