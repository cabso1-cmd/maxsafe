Specialized & Project Cargo

Unusual cargo and complex logistics call for surveyors who have actually handled them before. Our founders' global, six-decade background covers cargo most surveyors have never seen.

Project cargo, remote operations, and specialized transfers require judgment that only comes from direct, varied experience. MaxSafe brings that experience to the most complex marine cargo challenges.

What this service covers

  • Project cargo & breakbulk logistics surveys — surveying oversized, heavy-lift, and non-standard cargo.
  • Route surveys & packaging/storage recommendations — assessing transport routes and advising on packaging and storage.
  • Arctic, remote & sealift operations support — surveying support for challenging remote and seasonal sealift operations.
  • Ship-to-ship transfer surveys — surveying cargo transfers conducted vessel-to-vessel.
  • Offshore surveying — surveying support for offshore cargo and equipment operations.

Who this is for

Project cargo owners, logistics coordinators, and operators working in remote, Arctic, or offshore environments who need a surveyor with genuine experience in complex, non-standard cargo movements.
