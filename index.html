About MaxSafe

Two careers, one standard of independent surveying.

MaxSafe Services Ltd was founded in Ontario by two marine surveyors who have spent their careers on the docks, in the holds, and on the water — not behind a desk. Our senior founder entered the profession in the 1960s and built a truly global career across ports on nearly every continent. Together with a second principal surveyor who brings decades of specialized experience in this exact field, the two founders bring more than 100 years of combined, hands-on marine surveying experience to every engagement.
