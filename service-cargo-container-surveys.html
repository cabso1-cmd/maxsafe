Cargo & Container Surveys

Independent, on-site surveying of cargo and containers at every stage — from loading through discharge — so you have an accurate, defensible record of condition and quantity.

Whether you're a cargo owner, insurer, freight forwarder, or shipping line, an independent survey at the right moment protects everyone's interests. MaxSafe's surveyors attend in person to observe, document, and report — giving you a factual record you can rely on for shipping decisions, insurance purposes, or claims.

What this service covers

  • Loading & discharge surveys — on-site attendance to verify condition and quantity as cargo is loaded or discharged.
  • ISO container surveys — structural and condition surveys of shipping containers, including on/off-hire condition reports.
  • Breakbulk & project cargo surveys — surveying of non-containerized and oversized cargo.
  • Condition & damage surveys — documenting the physical condition of cargo and identifying any damage.
  • Loading, lashing & stowage supervision — oversight to confirm cargo is secured and stowed correctly.
  • Commodity tallying & quantity control — independent counts and measurement verification.
  • Pre-shipment quality & quantity inspections — verification before cargo departs origin.
  • Cargo contamination inspections — assessment where contamination is suspected or reported.

Who this is for

Cargo owners, importers/exporters, freight forwarders, shipping lines, and insurers who need an independent, on-the-record survey of cargo condition or quantity at any point in the shipping process.
