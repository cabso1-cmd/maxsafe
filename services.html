Marine surveying services built on real-world experience

MaxSafe Services Ltd provides independent, principal-led marine cargo surveying across five core areas. Select a service below for full details, or contact us to discuss your specific requirement.

1. Cargo & Container Surveys

Loading & discharge surveys, ISO container surveys, breakbulk & project cargo surveys, condition & damage surveys, loading/lashing/stowage supervision, commodity tallying and quantity control, pre-shipment quality & quantity inspections, and cargo contamination inspections.

View details →

2. Claims, Loss & Damage Investigation

Cargo damage & shortage claims investigation, loss adjustment, salvage & loss mitigation management, settlement recommendations, subrogation & recovery support, and P&I club / insurer liaison and claims support.

View details →

3. Vessel & Equipment Surveys

Vessel condition surveys, pre-purchase inspections, bunker (ROB) surveys, barge on/off-hire surveys, hull & machinery damage assessment, and terminal/port equipment safety audits.

View details →

4. Documentation & Trade Compliance

Bill of lading verification, letter of credit verification, freight forwarding coordination, and regulatory & risk compliance audits.

View details →

5. Specialized & Project Cargo

Project cargo & breakbulk logistics surveys, route surveys & packaging/storage recommendations, Arctic, remote & sealift operations support, ship-to-ship transfer surveys, and offshore surveying.

View details →
