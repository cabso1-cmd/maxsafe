Claims, Loss & Damage Investigation

When cargo arrives short or damaged, an independent, technically grounded investigation is what makes a claim defensible — for cargo owners and insurers alike.

MaxSafe investigates cargo loss and damage claims with the same rigor our founders have applied for decades — establishing what happened, when, and why, and providing recommendations that hold up under scrutiny from insurers, counsel, or the courts.

What this service covers

  • Cargo damage & shortage claims investigation — determining cause, extent, and timing of loss or damage.
  • Loss adjustment (cargo) — independent assessment to support claim resolution.
  • Salvage & loss mitigation management — coordinating salvage to minimize further loss.
  • Settlement recommendations — clear, evidence-based recommendations to support claim settlement.
  • Subrogation & recovery support — documentation to support recovery from responsible parties.
  • P&I club / insurer liaison & claims support — coordinating directly with insurers and P&I clubs throughout the process.

Who this is for

Insurers, P&I clubs, cargo owners, and legal counsel who need an independent investigation to support a cargo damage, shortage, or loss claim.
